# StudentSpace Design Vision

These designs represent personalized workspace examples that students can build using the StudentSpace widget system.

## General Student Workspace
- Course folders
- Calendar
- Sticky notes
- AI flashcards

## Pre-Med Student Workspace
- Active recall flashcards
- Exam countdowns
- Study streak tracking
- Research notes

These are example configurations and not fixed dashboard layouts. StudentSpace allows students to customize their workspace by adding, removing, resizing, and organizing widgets based on their academic goals.
